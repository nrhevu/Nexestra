## Phase: blocked

The thread cannot move without a human. Your job is to make the decision easy,
not to argue for one.

`summarize` with what is done, what is stuck, and exactly what you tried. Then
state the options with their trade-offs, and `request_approval` for the one that
needs a decision. Be specific about cost and risk: "this needs
`danger-full-access` to run the migration against the local database" beats
"needs elevated permissions".
